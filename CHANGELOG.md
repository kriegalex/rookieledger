# RookieLedger v0.2.0

- New: gathering. For every kind of vein and herb you mine or pick, the addon records how often, what it gave and
  where on the map you stood. `/ledger` counts the gathers.
- Gathering places are positions, so guild sync shares them an hour late and without a time: it never says where you
  are or just were.
- The site's summary lists the yield of each vein and herb and the number of places it was found at.

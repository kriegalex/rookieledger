# RookieLedger v0.2.0

- New: gathering. For every kind of vein and herb you mine or pick, the addon records how often, what it gave and
  where on the map you stood.
- New: monster loot. For every kind of monster you loot alone, the addon records the corpses looted, their coin and
  every item they held.
- Guild sync is gone. The addon no longer sends or receives anything in game: your record reaches the guild site
  through the companion app only, and other members' records are removed from your saved file.
- `/ledger` counts gathers and corpses; `/ledger sync` no longer exists.
- The site's summary lists gathering yields and places, and the most looted creatures.
